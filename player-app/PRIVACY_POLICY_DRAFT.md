# Orbit Player Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Orbit Player helps players discover participating poker clubs, running games, waitlists, memberships, and loyalty information.

## Information Collected

- Account details you enter, such as name, email, and phone number.
- Game preferences, stakes, availability, and home area.
- Club membership requests and waitlist requests.
- App sync identifiers generated by Firebase Authentication.
- Age-verification status. Stripe Identity collects and processes the government ID, date of birth, and matching selfie used for the check; Orbit does not store those source images or document details.

## How Information Is Used

- To create and maintain your player profile.
- To show relevant clubs and games.
- To send membership and waitlist requests to participating clubs.
- To sync loyalty and membership information.
- To limit game access, card-house purchases, hosting, registration, and check-in to verified players age 21 or older.

## Data Storage

The app stores your player profile locally on your device and syncs club-related data through Firebase services. Stripe Identity processes identity-verification materials and returns a verification result; Orbit stores only a Stripe session reference and a private age-eligibility result. Apple processes Player Premium purchases. Participating card houses process their real-world access products through Stripe Connect. Orbit receives entitlement and transaction-status information but not your full payment-card number.

## Location

The app currently uses a map-based home area selection. If precise location services are added later, the app should request permission before accessing device location.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account from Profile & Settings in the app. This deletes your player profile and sign-in and asks Stripe to redact the related Identity verification session. Stripe says completion of that redaction can take up to four days. Transactional or club request records may be retained when needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or participating-club operational records.
