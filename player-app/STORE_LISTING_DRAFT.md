## Full Description

Orbit Player helps poker players find the right game before they arrive. Browse participating clubs, see which games are running, check available seats, join waitlists, manage club memberships, and track loyalty progress by club.

Built for players who want less guessing and more playing:

- Discover live games by club and location.
- See available seats and waitlist activity.
- Join a club from your phone.
- Request a waitlist spot.
- Save game preferences and availability.
- Track club-specific loyalty status.

Orbit Player syncs with participating clubs using Orbit management tools.

## App Review Notes

Orbit Player is a discovery and operations companion for participating poker clubs. It does not accept wagers, hold gambling funds, sell entries into real-money games, determine game outcomes, or pay prizes. Membership and waitlist requests are operational messages to participating venues.

Player Premium is a digital auto-renewable subscription purchased through Apple In-App Purchase. It unlocks fit-score recommendations and player-hosted game listings. Use the Restore Apple Purchases action in Profile & Settings to restore an entitlement.

Any card-house checkout shown in the app is sold and fulfilled by the identified venue for a real-world service consumed outside the app. Orbit does not sell poker wagers or table stakes.

Provide a review account connected to populated production-review club data before submission.
