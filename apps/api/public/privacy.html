1. Scope and who is responsible

This Privacy Policy explains how Orbit Technologies LLC ("Orbit," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal data when you use the Services described in the Terms, communicate with us, attend an Orbit event, or interact with our marketing.

Orbit is generally the controller or business responsible for Player accounts, Orbit-operated websites and events, platform security, product analytics, and Orbit marketing. When a Venue uses Orbit Core to enter or manage its own customer, staff, operational, or transaction records, the Venue may be the controller or business and Orbit may process that data as its processor or service provider under contract. Contact the Venue for requests about Venue-controlled data. Orbit may still be independently responsible for account, security, billing, and platform-analytics data it uses for its own purposes.

This Policy does not govern a Venue's independent practices, gameplay or transactions conducted outside Orbit, employee and applicant data handled under separate notices, or third-party services linked from Orbit.

3. Sources of personal data

  • Directly from you, including account creation, registrations, permissions, support, payments, surveys, and User Content.
  • From participating Venues, organizers, sponsors, and their authorized staff, including schedules, memberships, check-ins, seats, attendance, and results.
  • Automatically from your browser, device, app, cookies, SDKs, logs, kiosks, or other connected equipment.
  • From service providers such as authentication, identity or age verification, payment, communications, analytics, security, customer support, mapping, and hosting providers.
  • From public sources, social media, referrals, ambassadors, event partners, and business contact databases, where lawful.
  • From other users when they connect with you, invite you, report content, or submit event or result information involving you.

4. How we use personal data

  • Provide accounts, authentication, profiles, discovery, registrations, waitlists, check-in, seating, notifications, statistics, leaderboards, community tools, support, and related features.
  • Enable Venues and organizers to operate events, manage staff and members, communicate with participants, support payouts and accounting, and understand performance.
  • Verify age, identity, eligibility, membership, location, or account ownership when needed.
  • Process subscriptions and transactions, support refunds and disputes, maintain financial records, and prevent chargeback abuse.
  • Personalize content and recommend Venues, games, tournaments, connections, or features.
  • Analyze adoption, engagement, attendance, retention, demand, operations, and product performance; create aggregated or deidentified insights.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, security incidents, policy violations, and unlawful activity.
  • Communicate service, event, security, policy, support, and optional marketing messages.
  • Develop, test, improve, and troubleshoot software, AI features, analytics, APIs, kiosks, devices, and future Orbit products.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements, protect rights and safety, respond to legal process, and establish or defend claims.

5. Location, age verification, and other sensitive data

Precise location, government ID data, account credentials, and some communications may be considered sensitive. We request device permission before collecting precise location and use it for nearby discovery, check-in or Venue verification, security, and features you select. You can disable location permission in device settings, but some features may not work.

Identity or age verification may be performed by a specialized provider. Where practical, Orbit receives a verification result, age band, or limited audit record rather than retaining a full government ID image. Do not upload government IDs through ordinary support or community channels unless we specifically direct you to an approved secure process.

We do not use sensitive data to infer characteristics unrelated to providing or securing the Services. We do not sell precise geolocation, government ID information, account passwords, or payment-card credentials. Where law requires consent for sensitive-data processing, we request it through a separate, specific choice rather than relying only on this Policy.

6. AI and automated processing

Orbit may use algorithms, AI, or machine learning to rank or recommend events, personalize experiences, summarize or assist support, generate operational analytics, and identify possible fraud or abuse. Inputs may include account, event, usage, preference, location, and Venue data, as appropriate. Orbit does not use these tools as the sole basis for decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Venue staff remain responsible for gameplay, seating, eligibility, discipline, prize, and employment decisions.

7. How we disclose personal data

7.1 Venues, organizers, and users

When you register, join a waitlist, check in, participate, connect, or use a Venue feature, we disclose information needed for that interaction. This may include your name or username, contact details, profile, membership or verification status, registration, seat, attendance, results, and relevant communications. Venues may use the information under their own notices and legal obligations.

Profile, leaderboard, achievement, friend, community, result, or event information may be visible to the audience shown in the feature. Review privacy settings before posting. Information shared publicly can be copied by others.

7.2 Service providers

We disclose data to vendors that provide hosting, databases, authentication, identity or age verification, payments, communications, customer support, analytics, crash reporting, security, mapping, content moderation, and professional services. They may process data only for contracted purposes and subject to appropriate obligations.

7.3 Advertising and measurement

We may use advertising, social-media, attribution, and measurement partners to promote Orbit and measure campaigns. Depending on the technology and applicable law, disclosure of identifiers, device or online activity, approximate location, and inferences to these partners may be considered a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising even when no money changes hands. You may opt out as described in Section 12. We do not knowingly sell or share personal data of anyone under 18 for targeted advertising.

7.4 Legal, safety, and business transfers

We may disclose data when reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process; protect users, Venues, Orbit, or the public; investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; enforce agreements; or establish or defend legal claims. Data may also be disclosed as part of due diligence, financing, merger, acquisition, restructuring, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of the business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal requirements.

7.5 At your direction

We disclose data when you request or authorize it, such as connecting an account, sharing a profile, registering with a Venue, participating in a promotion, or using a third-party integration.

8. Cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies

Websites and apps may use cookies, local storage, pixels, mobile SDKs, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, enable features, measure traffic and campaigns, diagnose errors, prevent fraud, and personalize content. Browser and device settings can limit some technologies. Where required, we provide a consent or privacy-choice tool.

We process legally recognized opt-out preference signals, such as Global Privacy Control, where applicable law requires us to do so. Signals generally apply to the browser or device that sends them and may not apply after you log in unless linked to your account.

9. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Retention depends on the data's nature, account and contract status, Venue instructions, transaction and event history, legal limitation periods, tax and accounting duties, safety and fraud risk, dispute needs, and technical backup cycles.

  • Account and profile data is generally kept while the account is active and for a limited period after closure to complete deletion, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or comply with law.
  • Event, result, and transaction records may be retained longer when needed for Venue operations, historical statistics, contractual records, accounting, tax, integrity, or legal claims.
  • Security logs, diagnostics, support records, and marketing preferences are retained for periods appropriate to their operational or legal purpose.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule. Data may remain in backups until that cycle completes and will not be restored for ordinary use after a valid deletion request.

We may retain aggregated or deidentified information that cannot reasonably be linked to you. We maintain measures intended to prevent reidentification where required by law.

10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed for the nature of the data and our Services. Measures may include access controls, encryption in transit, credential protection, logging, vendor review, secure development, backups, and incident response. No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for protecting your credentials and promptly reporting suspected misuse.

11. Your choices

  • Account and profile: review and update available information in account settings.
  • Location: change app permission in device settings; approximate location may still be inferred from IP address.
  • Push notifications: adjust app or device notification settings.
  • Marketing email: use the unsubscribe link. Service and security messages may continue.
  • Text messages: reply STOP where supported; message and data rates may apply.
  • Cookies and targeted advertising: use our privacy-choice tool when available, device settings, or a legally recognized opt-out signal.
  • Public and social features: use available audience, connection, blocking, and deletion controls.

12. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live and whether a law applies to Orbit, you may have rights to confirm processing; access or obtain a portable copy; correct; delete; opt out of targeted advertising, sale, sharing, or certain profiling; limit certain uses of sensitive personal data; withdraw consent; and receive equal service without unlawful discrimination.

12.1 How to submit a request

Use available in-app privacy controls, email privacy@orbitpoker.com with the subject "Privacy Request," or call 346-434-1402. Describe the right and the account, email, or phone involved. You do not need to create a new account. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify identity and authority. If we cannot verify a request, we may deny or limit it as law permits.

An authorized agent may submit a request where law permits. We may require proof of authorization and may ask you to verify your identity directly. Parents or guardians should contact us if they believe a minor's data was collected.

12.2 Appeals

If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or emailing privacy@orbitpoker.com with the subject "Privacy Appeal." Include enough information to identify the original request and explain the appeal. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and, when required, explain how to contact the appropriate regulator.

12.3 Opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising

Use the privacy-choice tool in the Services, send a recognized opt-out preference signal where supported and required, or email privacy@orbitpoker.com with the subject "Opt Out." We do not sell personal data for money. As explained in Section 7.3, some advertising or measurement disclosures may be treated as sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under state law.

13. Additional information for California residents

In the preceding 12 months, Orbit may have collected the categories described in Section 2 from the sources in Section 3, used them for the purposes in Section 4, and disclosed them to the recipients in Section 7. California law may classify them as identifiers; customer records; commercial information; internet or electronic network activity; geolocation; audio, electronic, visual, or similar information; professional or employment-related information for business contacts; inferences; and sensitive personal information such as precise geolocation, account credentials, or government ID data handled for verification.

Orbit may have "sold" or "shared" identifiers, internet or electronic network activity, approximate location, and inferences through advertising or measurement technologies as those terms are defined by California law. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that trigger a right to limit unless we provide the required notice and choice. Orbit does not offer a financial incentive for personal information unless separate program terms and a notice describe it.

California residents may request to know, access, correct, or delete personal information; opt out of sale or sharing; limit qualifying sensitive-personal-information uses; and receive non-discriminatory treatment, subject to legal exceptions. Submit requests as described in Section 12.

14. Children and age-restricted services

Orbit is an adult service and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 and do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create a Player account. If we learn that an ineligible minor provided personal data, we will take reasonable steps to delete or restrict it. Contact privacy@orbitpoker.com if you believe this occurred.

A Venue may impose a higher age requirement. Age verification reduces but cannot eliminate false information; parents and guardians should not allow minors to use an adult's account.

15. International access

Orbit is based in the United States and the Services are presently designed primarily for U.S. users and Venues. If you access the Services elsewhere, data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where providers operate. We use transfer measures required by applicable law when necessary.

16. Third-party services

Third-party Venues, payment processors, identity-verification providers, maps, social platforms, app stores, and linked sites operate under their own privacy notices. Review those notices before providing data. Orbit is not responsible for an independent third party's practices, though our contracts may require service providers acting for us to protect data.

17. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Services, laws, and practices change. We will post the updated Policy and revise the date above. For material changes, we will provide additional notice appropriate to the change. If law requires consent, we will request it before applying the new practice.
