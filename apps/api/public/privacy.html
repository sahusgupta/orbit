Orbit Player

Privacy Policy

Effective August 3, 2026

Orbit Player helps players discover participating card houses, manage a shared player profile, request club membership, join game waitlists, and view membership and play activity.

Information we collect

We may collect account identifiers, contact information, profile and game preferences, approximate home area, club memberships, waitlist requests, play and transaction history provided by participating clubs, device diagnostics, and purchase entitlement status.

How we use information

We use this information to operate and secure accounts, personalize discovery, synchronize player-authorized requests with participating clubs, provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain subscription features.

Service providers

Orbit uses service providers including Firebase for account and data services, Google Maps for maps, Stripe for eligible real-world venue services and identity verification, RevenueCat for purchase entitlement management, and Apple for App Store purchases. Those providers process information under their own policies.

Sharing and retention

Participating clubs receive the profile, membership, waitlist, and play information needed to provide their services. Orbit does not sell personal information. We retain information only as reasonably needed for service operation, security, legal obligations, and user-requested history.

Your choices

You may update profile information and request account deletion from Profile & Settings in the app. Account deletion removes the Orbit player account subject to legal, fraud-prevention, and transaction-record requirements.

Age and real-money play

Orbit Player is intended for adults aged 21 and older. Orbit does not accept wagers, hold table stakes, determine game outcomes, or pay prizes.
