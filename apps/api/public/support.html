Contact support

Call 346-434-1402. Include the name and phone number associated with your Orbit Player account and the participating club involved, if applicable.

Account deletion

You can request deletion directly from Profile & Settings inside Orbit Player. If you cannot access the app, contact support using the phone number above.

Purchases

Use Restore Apple Purchases in Profile & Settings if an active Player Premium entitlement does not appear. Card-house services are sold and fulfilled by the identified venue.
