Every room decision starts with the same live state.

Orbit carries demand, player context, seating, table activity, tournaments, and closeout into one connected operating record.

01

Demand and waitlists

Track interest, confirmation, arrival, seating, and departure with the requested game attached.

02

Tables and seating

Open games, place players in specific seats, record movement, and keep current table state visible.

03

Player records

Keep membership, visit, preference, transaction, and room context attached to the player record.

04

Tournaments

Create structures, register players, draw tables, run the clock, and manage payouts from one system.

05

Closeout and reporting

Review table counts, exceptions, staff sign-off, and operational history before producing the night report.

06

Player connection

Publish participating room information and route authenticated membership and waitlist requests back to staff.
