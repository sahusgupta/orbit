Product

A connected workflow for live poker rooms.

Orbit gives floor staff, managers, and players a clear path from expressed demand to a running table, with the operating record carried through the night.

01

Demand and waitlists

Record who is interested, confirmed, arrived, seated, or no longer waiting. Staff see the exact game and arrival context needed to act.

02

Tables and seating

Open tables, place players in specific seats, track movements, and keep table status visible from the floor view.

03

Player and membership records

Keep contact, membership, visit, preference, and transaction context together so staff do not rebuild the same record each shift.

04

Tournaments

Create structures, register and check in players, draw tables, operate the clock, and manage payouts from the same system.

05

Closeout and reporting

Review table counts, exceptions, staff sign-off, and operational history before producing the night report.

06

Player connection

Orbit Player publishes participating room information and routes player-authorized membership and waitlist requests back to staff.
