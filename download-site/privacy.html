Orbit Player Privacy Policy

Orbit Player helps players discover participating poker clubs, view live games, and send membership and waitlist requests.

Information we collect

We collect profile information you provide, including your name, email address, optional phone number, home area, game preferences, stakes preferences, and availability. We also process club memberships, waitlist requests, loyalty information, private-game listings, and account identifiers.

How we use information

We use this information to operate your account, personalize game discovery, synchronize participating club information, process requests you initiate, provide premium features, prevent abuse, and support the service.

Service providers

Orbit uses Firebase for authentication and cloud data synchronization, Google Maps for map presentation, and Apple for App Store purchases. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Purchases

Apple processes Player Premium subscriptions. Orbit receives subscription entitlement and transaction-status information but does not receive your full payment-card number.

Location

You may enter a home area for distance-based discovery. The current app does not request continuous background location access.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your Orbit Player account from Profile & Settings. This deletes your player profile and sign-in. Transactional or club request records may be retained when needed for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or the participating club’s legitimate operational records.

Contact
